Concept for Groww

SIP Recovery Helper

For first-time Groww SIP investors when an instalment fails, gets stuck, or may fail soon.
Concept prototype for Groww, made for a product case study. Not an official Groww product and not made by Groww. Not investment advice. Do not type your PAN, bank account number, OTP, PIN or screenshots.

What happened?

Try a scenario

For testers. Pick a situation, imagine it is happening to you, and use the helper as you would. At the end, three quick questions. Nothing you type is stored; only your choices and the time taken.

Quality check (the gate)

The AI is tested on 25 situations written from real Groww SIP posts, plus tricky ones (asking which fund to buy, sharing a PAN, angry messages). AI answers go live only if at least 90% are right and there are zero rule breaks. Otherwise the helper shows standard (non-AI) help.

How it works

  1. You say what happened by tapping a choice and/or typing in any language.
  2. Personal data is removed first. PAN, account numbers, UPI IDs, OTPs and similar are stripped before anything is sent to the AI or saved.
  3. The AI (Google Gemini, free tier) works out which of 4 situations it is, puts the options in the order that fits your case, and writes a short plain summary in your language.
  4. Facts and steps are fixed text from public sources (below). The AI cannot change them.
  5. Every AI sentence is checked for fund names, advice, return predictions, fear or urgency words, and requests for personal data. Anything that fails is removed.
  6. You choose. Nothing happens to your SIP from here; every option tells you where to do it in the app. Stopping is always shown as a neutral option.

Where this would sit in Groww

  • Failed-debit alert: when an instalment fails, the notification opens this helper with the situation already filled in.
  • My SIPs: a "Something went wrong with this instalment?" link on each SIP.
  • 3 days before the SIP date: if the person has said money is tight, a gentle "need to change this month?" entry point.

In this prototype you open it directly and describe the situation yourself, because it has no access to real Groww accounts.

What it will never do

Recommend or name funds, predict returns, push you with fear or urgency, or ask for PAN, account numbers or screenshots.
